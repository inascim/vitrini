## The challenge

The clinic relied almost entirely on ads to attract patients, and its website didn't appear in searches for its specialties in the area.

## The strategy

We fixed the site's technical issues, optimised each location's Google Maps profile and published one article a week answering patients' most-searched questions.

## Results

Within six months, organic traffic more than tripled and became the source of most bookings.
