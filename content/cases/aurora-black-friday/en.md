## The challenge

Aurora was coming off a Black Friday with high acquisition costs and sales concentrated on a single day. The goal was to grow revenue without relying on aggressive discounts.

## The strategy

We split the campaign into three phases: a **warm-up** with content and lead capture, **conversion** with exclusive offers for the customer base, and **post-purchase** to drive repeat sales.

- Lookalike audiences built from the best customers
- Short-form video creatives tested every week
- A VIP list with early access via WhatsApp

## Results

The monthly revenue target was reached in two days, at the lowest cost per acquisition in the brand's history.
