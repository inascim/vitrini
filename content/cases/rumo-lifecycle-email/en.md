## The challenge

Many users downloaded the Rumo app but dropped off before connecting their first bank account.

## The strategy

We designed a seven-day welcome journey with messages triggered by each user's behaviour, plus a win-back campaign for anyone inactive for more than 30 days.

## Results

First-week activation grew by 22%, and the journey became the model for the company's other products.
