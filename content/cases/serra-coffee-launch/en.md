## The challenge

A family-owned roaster wanted to launch its first specialty coffee line to a young audience that didn't know the brand yet.

## The strategy

We built a story around where the beans come from, told by small farmers and by **coffee creators** who tried the products before launch. All content was designed vertical-video first.

## Results

The launch batch sold out in one week and the brand's profile tripled in size.
