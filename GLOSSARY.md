# Glossary

**Template** — The reusable portfolio project itself, meant to be forked. It contains no one's personal content beyond example placeholders.

**Owner** — The person a portfolio site is about, who maintains their own fork of the Template. Owners are not assumed to be developers; they may edit content by hand or hand documents to an AI agent to do it.

**Fork** — One Owner's copy of the Template, published as their site. Forks are independent; they do not receive Template updates automatically.

**Case** — One piece of showcased work (a campaign, client engagement, design, or software project). Has a cover, a summary, and optionally a client, metrics, and a full write-up. The label shown to visitors ("Campaigns", "Work", "Projects") is chosen by the Owner; "Case" is the canonical name.

**Tag** — A category label on a Case (e.g. "SEO", "Social"), translated per Locale. Visitors can filter Cases by Tag.

**Theme** — The visual identity an Owner picks: a preset plus their own accent color, font pair, and light/dark preference. Layout is not part of the Theme.

**Metric** — A short, quantified result attached to a Case (e.g. "+42% CTR", "1.2M reach").

**Locale** — A language the site is published in (e.g. `pt`, `en`, `es`). An Owner chooses which Locales their site supports; every piece of Owner content can exist once per Locale.

**Default Locale** — The one Locale an Owner designates as primary. When content is missing in another Locale, visitors see the Default Locale's version, marked as untranslated, rather than nothing.

**Section** — A distinct part of the home page: Hero, Cases, About, Services, Clients, Testimonials, Contact. An Owner can switch any Section except Hero on or off.

**Service** — Something the Owner offers to prospective clients (e.g. "SEO", "Paid media"). Distinct from a Case: a Service is what they *can* do, a Case is evidence of what they *did*.

**Testimonial** — A quote about the Owner's work from a named person, with their role and company.
